## Why

CommandKey AI ships this software to its clients from a reviewed, pinned mirror. The changes below (1) make the Python environment reproducible (a committed lockfile, `uv sync --frozen` that refuses to launch when it fails, one pinned and hash-checked `uv` used by absolute path), (2) pin the speech-model weights to reviewed Hugging Face commits and fail closed when a pin cannot be applied, (3) give Windows a scripted installer instead of improvised steps, (4) make every install and update path land on the reviewed release tag and refuse any other download source, (5) close the by-voice route into auto-approve on client machines, (6) bound the on-disk transcript log and keep it user-only where the OS supports it, and (7) replace the author's community and marketing material with the CommandKey AI support pointer (author attribution and license credit are kept).
